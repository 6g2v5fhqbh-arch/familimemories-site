FamiliMemories

Privacy policy · Effective October 15, 2026

FamiliMemories shows the photos and videos from your library that were taken on the same date in past years. Everything happens on your device: no account, no server, no ads, no tracking. We collect no data.

Your photos and videos

With your permission, the app reads your photo library directly on the device to find today’s memories. Your photos are not copied to a server or analyzed off the device. They only leave your iPhone when you choose to share them, to the app and recipient you pick.

If you allow adding photos, the app only saves to your library the images or videos you choose to save from the share sheet.

Photo locations

When you open a photo that contains a location, its coordinates are sent to Apple’s geocoding service to display the place name (city, country). This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Contacts and Calendars (optional)

If you turn on birthdays, the app reads birthdays from your Contacts and Calendars to show them under the day’s date. This information is read on demand, is not stored by the app and does not leave the device.

Data stored on the device

The app keeps locally:

  • your daily streak, badges, tickets and mini-game results;
  • your settings (daily reminder and its time, sounds, birthday display);
  • a daily summary for the widget and Apple Watch: your streak, the number of memories and a few small thumbnails of today’s photos.

The summary is sent to your Apple Watch directly over the connection between your devices. This data is part of your iPhone backups managed by Apple. Deleting the app erases it. We cannot read it.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on the device. No notification is sent from a server.

What we don’t collect

No email, name, Apple ID, device location, advertising identifier, usage analytics or crash reports sent to us. No third-party advertising or analytics kits are included in the app.

Children

The app is made for families. It collects no personal data and builds no profiles.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, as well as the App Store privacy section when required.
