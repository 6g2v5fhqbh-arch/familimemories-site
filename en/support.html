Support · App Store

No memories show up

  • The app shows photos and videos taken on the same date in past years. Some days simply have none: tap the date at the top of the screen to pick another one.
  • Check access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › FamiliMemories. With limited access, only the selected photos appear.
  • Photos hidden in the Photos app are never shown.
  • If you use iCloud Photos with Optimize Storage, full-resolution images download from iCloud, so a connection is needed.

Your streak

A day counts toward your streak when you scroll through today’s memories to the bottom of the “Today” page. Badges unlock throughout the year; some stay secret until you earn them.

Reminder, sounds and birthdays

Tap the bell icon at the top right to turn on the daily reminder and choose its time, mute the projector sounds, or show birthdays from your Contacts and Calendars. Sounds stay muted when your iPhone is in silent mode.

The intermission and tickets

Tap the ticket icon at the top right to open the 5 mini-games. Each game earns a limited number of tickets per day; once the cap is reached, you can keep playing for fun. When today has no memories, the games use random photos from your library.

Widget

The “On this day” widget shows today’s memories as a mosaic, then your streak once you’ve seen them. The widget updates when you open the app on your iPhone during the day.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your streak, badges and settings are stored on the device and are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Deleting the app erases them. Your photos stay in your photo library.
